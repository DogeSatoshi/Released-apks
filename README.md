# Astra-Axiom Sentinel

### Real-Time Crypto Market Intelligence for Android

Astra-Axiom Sentinel is a lightweight Android market-intelligence application built to provide a clear, fast view of live cryptocurrency market conditions without turning the screen into an overloaded trading terminal.

Sentinel is designed around **market observation and situational awareness**: pull current market data, present it in a compact interface, and give the user the information needed to understand what is happening in the market at a glance.

## What Is Sentinel?

Sentinel is an Android-based cryptocurrency market monitoring tool developed under the **Astra-Axiom Intelligence Systems** platform.

The application connects to live market data and presents a focused snapshot of market conditions, with particular emphasis on **order-book and market microstructure information**.

Rather than attempting to execute trades or make decisions on behalf of the user, Sentinel is intended to function as a **market observation instrument** — giving the user direct visibility into the data before making their own decisions.

---

## Core Capabilities

### 📊 Live Market Snapshot

Sentinel provides a compact view of current market information so that important conditions can be assessed without navigating through multiple services.

### 📖 Order-Book Visibility

The application provides visibility into the current order-book environment, allowing users to observe available bids and asks and the relationship between them.

Order-book information can be useful for identifying changes in liquidity, shifts in buying or selling pressure, and short-term changes in market structure.

### ⚡ Lightweight Android Interface

Sentinel was built specifically for Android rather than attempting to reproduce a full desktop trading terminal on a mobile screen.

The interface is designed around rapid information retrieval: open the application, inspect the current market state, and move on.

### 🌐 Binance Market Data

Sentinel uses publicly available market data provided by Binance for its market-information functionality.

The application does not require users to provide Binance trading credentials for its market-data functionality.

## Designed for Observation, Not Gambling

Sentinel is intended to provide **information**, not financial instructions.

It does not promise profitable trades, guaranteed returns, or predictive market accuracy. Market data can change rapidly, and order-book information represents a snapshot of market conditions rather than a guarantee of future price movement.

The purpose of Sentinel is simple:

> **Give the user better visibility into the market before they act.**

---

## Data Source

Market information displayed by Sentinel is sourced from Binance's publicly available market-data infrastructure.

Data availability, latency, market conditions, and exchange-side changes may affect what is displayed by the application.

Sentinel does not control Binance's infrastructure or guarantee the availability or accuracy of third-party exchange data.

---

## Privacy & Security

Sentinel's market-data functionality is designed around publicly available exchange information and does not require access to a user's Binance trading account for normal market observation.

**Never provide private API keys, passwords, seed phrases, or wallet recovery information to Sentinel or to any third party claiming to represent the application.**
